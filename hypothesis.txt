This is a Wishlist to Purchase Analysis for Myntra.
MY CORE PROBLEM:
Understand why users add/save fashion products to their wishlist but do not purchase them within 30 days, and identify an opportunity for an AI-powered solution to improve wishlist-to-purchase conversion.

I have a dataset of Myntra/e-commerce customer reviews. Analyse the dataset specifically for this problem.

IMPORTANT:
Do NOT simply give me generic sentiment analysis such as "positive/negative", and do NOT focus primarily on technical issues like app crashes, payment failures, or checkout latency unless they are clearly connected to the user's decision not to purchase a product.

I need PRODUCT DISCOVERY RESEARCH.

STEP 1 — CLEAN THE DATA
- Remove duplicates.
- Remove empty/irrelevant reviews.
- Keep the original review text.
- Do not invent, rewrite, or fabricate customer statements.
- Report the total number of reviews before and after cleaning.

STEP 2 — IDENTIFY RELEVANT REVIEWS
Classify every review into one of these categories:

A. DIRECTLY RELEVANT:
The review explicitly mentions or strongly indicates:
- wishlist
- saved items
- save for later
- shortlisted products
- added but did not buy
- waiting to purchase
- liked the product but didn't purchase
- comparing products before buying

B. INDIRECTLY RELEVANT:
The review does not mention wishlist, but describes a barrier that could reasonably prevent a user from purchasing a fashion product, such as:
- price too high
- waiting for discount/sale
- uncertainty about quality
- uncertainty about size/fit
- uncertainty about appearance
- lack of trust
- poor/insufficient reviews
- difficulty comparing products
- too many similar choices
- difficulty discovering the right product
- product availability
- delivery concern
- return/exchange concern
- lack of useful recommendations
- uncertainty about whether the product is worth buying

C. NOT RELEVANT:
Issues that have no meaningful connection to wishlist-to-purchase behaviour, such as generic app crashes, login issues, unrelated technical problems, etc.

DO NOT assume that every indirect issue causes wishlist abandonment. Mark it as a potential barrier, not a proven cause.

STEP 3 — EXTRACT STRUCTURED DATA FOR EVERY RELEVANT REVIEW

For each relevant review, create these fields:

1. Review ID
2. Original review text
3. Relevance:
   - Direct
   - Indirect
4. Sentiment:
   - Positive
   - Neutral
   - Negative
5. User behaviour:
   - Wishlisted/Saved
   - Considered but didn't buy
   - Compared products
   - Waiting
   - Purchased
   - Other
   - Unknown
6. Purchase barrier:
   - Price
   - Discount waiting
   - Size/Fit
   - Quality uncertainty
   - Product appearance
   - Reviews/ratings
   - Trust
   - Choice overload
   - Product comparison
   - Product discovery
   - Availability
   - Delivery
   - Return/Exchange
   - Recommendations
   - Other
   - Unknown
7. Root cause/theme
8. Evidence/explanation from the review
9. Confidence:
   - High
   - Medium
   - Low

IMPORTANT:
Only classify a behaviour or barrier when there is evidence in the review. If it cannot be determined, use "Unknown". Do not guess.

STEP 4 — FIND PATTERNS

After classifying the reviews, calculate:

- Total reviews analysed
- Total directly relevant reviews
- Total indirectly relevant reviews
- Total relevant reviews
- Percentage of total reviews that are relevant
- Frequency and percentage of each purchase barrier
- Frequency and percentage of each root-cause/theme
- Frequency of direct wishlist behaviour
- Frequency of each user behaviour

For each major theme, provide 2–5 representative ORIGINAL review examples.

Do not fabricate quotes.

STEP 5 — CREATE PM HYPOTHESES

Based ONLY on the strongest evidence in the dataset, generate 3–5 testable product hypotheses.

Use this format:

H1:
We believe that ______ is a major reason users do not purchase wishlisted products because ______.

Evidence:
- Number of relevant reviews
- Percentage
- Supporting review examples

What needs validation:
What we still need to learn from real users.

Do NOT label a hypothesis "validated" based only on reviews.

The reviews are SECONDARY RESEARCH.
They generate hypotheses.
They do NOT prove the hypotheses.

STEP 6 — PRIORITIZE THE HYPOTHESES

Create a table with:

Hypothesis | Evidence volume | User impact | Confidence | Priority

Use High/Medium/Low rather than inventing precise scores unless the data supports them.

STEP 7 — PRODUCT OPPORTUNITY

Based on the strongest hypotheses, identify the top 1–2 product opportunities for Myntra.

For each opportunity explain:

- User problem
- Evidence
- Likely user need
- Why it matters to wishlist-to-purchase conversion
- What should be validated through user interviews
- Possible AI solution direction

IMPORTANT:
Do NOT jump directly to an AI solution.
First establish the problem and evidence.

Give me these sections in the final output (do not use numbers, just bold headers):
